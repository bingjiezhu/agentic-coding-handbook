\clearpage\section*{Twelve Chapters at a Glance}\label{summary}\markboth{Summary}{}
\lede{Clarify goals and trade-offs, preserve context in project files, and leave a verifiable result after each increment.}
\vspace{3mm}
\begingroup
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{8mm}L{43mm}Y@{}}
\toprule Ch. & Topic & Core idea and action\\\midrule
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{01}} & Define an actionable task & \textbf{Define outcomes and trade-offs.} Discuss and write the spec; people review scope and observable acceptance criteria.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{02}} & Connect project files & \textbf{Keep context in files.} Index requirements, evidence and progress; reread after compaction or a new session.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{03}} & Understand before changing & \textbf{Research before architecture.} Compare reuse, adaptation and a new build; verify sources, fit and key unknowns.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{04}} & Use Spec Kit & \textbf{Keep requirements connected to execution.} Maintain specs, plans and tasks; checked boxes do not prove completion.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{05}} & Build in small steps & \textbf{Make each round verifiable and recoverable.} Save a baseline, connect real behavior and give precise feedback.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{06}} & Check actual use & \textbf{Derive expectations from requirements.} Verify full workflows and saved data; separate pass, fail and unverified.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{07}} & Diagnose before editing & \textbf{Make failures reusable evidence.} Record hypotheses, conditions and results; keep regression checks after repairs.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{08}} & Split independent work & \textbf{Parallelize independent tasks.} Specify inputs and scope; reconcile evidence and measure cost before scaling.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{09}} & Review independently & \textbf{Verify review findings too.} Read requirements and the current version; report triggers and evidence; recheck fixes.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{10}} & Resume long tasks & \textbf{Advance a queue of specs.} Define dependencies, acceptance and stop conditions; save results and verify before resuming.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{11}} & Reuse rules and Skills & \textbf{Keep methods that repeatedly help.} Rules hold conventions, Skills hold methods and scripts run fixed steps; test actual reuse.\\[5pt]
\textcolor{accent}{\textsf{12}} & Pilot, then scale & \textbf{Validate the process first.} Pilot common and difficult cases, track every item, resume gaps and measure time and rework.\\[5pt]
\bottomrule\end{tabularx}
\endgroup\par
\vspace{5mm}
\textbf{The thread through all twelve chapters:} People own goals, judgment and final acceptance. AI executes within an agreed scope. Files preserve requirements, decisions and experience; evidence shows what is complete. Organize the project before adding automation.
