\clearpage\section*{From an Idea to a Usable Result}\label{guide}\markboth{Working framework}{}
\lede{Describe a goal in natural language. Let a coding agent read the project, edit files, run checks and improve the result. The agent repeatedly uses tools and chooses its next step from what it observes.}
\diagram{p_route}
\topic{One workflow, with methods added as needed}
Clarify the goal, research existing solutions, then ask AI to implement, check and revise. Keep requirements, evidence and progress in project files; add collaboration and automation when useful. Understand what each method solves and when to use it. AI can help look up the tool-specific steps.

Several independent tasks & Investigate in parallel; coordinate edits and review important results independently\\

\topic{How to use the shaded prompt boxes}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Find and copy.} Each large shaded box in a chapter contains a prompt. Choose one for your task and copy all the text below its title.
\item \textbf{Replace the brackets.} Fill in placeholders such as ``[goal]'' with your requirements, for example: ``Add a to-do item and keep it after a refresh.''
\item \textbf{Paste and send.} Open the project in Codex or Claude Code, send the completed prompt in the chat, then run and check the result as directed.
\end{enumerate}
\topic{Where human judgment matters}
People decide goals, trade-offs and whether to accept the result; AI compares options, executes and provides evidence. Here, \emph{taste} means understanding users and setting priorities. A demo may emphasize presentation; a daily-use tool may prioritize reliability. Meet essential requirements before balancing experience, cost and speed. Express preferences through scenarios, examples and observable criteria, and check whether those criteria fit the task.
